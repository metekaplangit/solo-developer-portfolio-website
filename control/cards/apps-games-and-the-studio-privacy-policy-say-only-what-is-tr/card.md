The owner asked for a review of every area a reviewer of Waypost Words could
open. It is in `review-0001-2026-09-29.md` beside this card: 18 areas checked
against the game's source, its listing batch and App Review Guidelines 1.5,
2.3, 5.1.1(i) and 5.1.4, read 2026-09-29, plus every route fetched live.

Two sentences failed it, and this card fixes both. Apps & Games called itself
"Everything the studio has shipped" with a game in development on it, and said
each product has "its own support page" when there is one support page; it now
reads "Everything the studio makes, released or on its way. Each product has its
own privacy page, and one person answers support for all of them." The
site-wide privacy policy said "some of them do collect data"; none of the three
does, so it now says each has its own policy, and its date moves to today.

A built-output test forbids all three phrases; it was seen red against the
build before the change. The rest of what the review found is below its bar or
the owner's to do, and is listed in the report.
