The 2 fit cells on each product page held a sentence with a dash-led second
half — "and deliberately stops there", "Sole Focus deliberately does none of
those" — which said the same thing twice. Each is now 1 plain sentence:

- Magic Notes: "You want to see the working behind budgets, quotes and loans." /
  "You need a spreadsheet or a programmable calculator."
- Sole Focus: "You want a calm, native Mac timer that waits in your menu bar." /
  "You need a website blocker, team tracking or sync across devices."

Measured in the built site: at 1920 and 1536 the cells went from 3 and 3 lines
(Magic Notes) and 2 and 2 (Sole Focus) to 1 line each; at 414 from 3 or 4
lines to 2. The 3 bad breaks they had at the standard sizes are gone. The test
phrases "calm, native Mac timer" and "website blocker" are kept.
