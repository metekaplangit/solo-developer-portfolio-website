# The Wander Words privacy policy speaks to a general audience

bump: patch
screen: @privacy-wander-words
unproven: the changed sentence on the live site, checked by fetching it after the push

The Wander Words policy's children's section said the game was "for players of
all ages, children included". The owner plans AdMob ads and Apple in-app
purchases for a later update, and under the amended COPPA rule a service that
presents itself as for children is treated as directed at them: third-party
advertising then needs separate parental consent and is limited to child-safe
ad requests. The section now says the game is made for a general audience and is
not directed at children, which is how word games of its kind present
themselves, and it keeps the statement that no personal data is collected from
anyone. The owner decided this on 2026-09-22.

Nothing else on the page moved. A comment beside the override records why, and
that the App Store age rating answers have to agree with it.
