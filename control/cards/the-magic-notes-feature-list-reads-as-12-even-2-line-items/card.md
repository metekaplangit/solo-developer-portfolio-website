# The Magic Notes feature list reads as 12 even 2-line items

bump: patch
screen: @magic-notes

Plan 0005, card E: piece 5 of text-project 0001.

The "What it does" list on the Magic Notes page was the longest on the site:
12 items of 47 to 77 characters that took 2 to 4 lines each, so the ruled list
looked torn. Each item is rewritten to lead with its key words and say one
thing, 40 to 53 characters, 88 words in all against 128. Nothing the list
claimed was added to; "Spotlight indexing that is off until you ask" became
"opt-in Spotlight indexing", which says the same thing without reading as if
Zen mode and Shortcuts were off too. Still 12 items, a multiple of 4.

Measured in headless Chrome on the built page: every item takes 2 lines at
1920, 1440 and 390, with no bad break and no page overflow (2 items take 3
lines at 320, which is judged for fit only).
