The home page lists the first sentence of each of Magic Notes' first 3
sections. They were "The engine keeps only values it can represent exactly.",
"It reads the arithmetic people actually write." and "Exchange rates are
yours." — 54, 46 and 25 characters, and none said what a reader gets. They now
read "Every answer is exact, or clearly marked with ≈.", "Money, dates, units
and loans, in plain sentences." and "You set each exchange rate, and the date
it was true." — 48, 50 and 53 characters, 1 line each at 1920, 1536 and 414.

The rest of each paragraph was reworded only as far as the new opening needed;
every claim it made is still there. Measured in the built site, the 3
paragraphs on the Magic Notes page had 3 bad breaks at the standard sizes (a
stranded "Anything approximate" at 1920 and 1536, 2 stranded phrase starts at
414) and now have none. Neither page scrolls sideways at 320.
