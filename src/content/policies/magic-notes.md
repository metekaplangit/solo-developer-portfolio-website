Magic Notes keeps everything on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no
ads — nothing you write in the app ever leaves your device, and the app makes no
network requests at all.

The only internet addresses in the app are the links in Settings that open this
studio's own pages — this policy, the product page, and support — in your
browser. Opening one is your choice, and the app sends nothing with it.

If the app ever stops after an unexpected quit, it may show you a local notice
naming the crash report macOS itself wrote. That report belongs to macOS, and
Magic Notes neither reads it, keeps it, nor sends it anywhere.
