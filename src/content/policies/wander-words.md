Wander Words keeps everything on your iPhone. The game as it is built today has
no account, no analytics, no advertising and no purchases, and it plays without
connecting to the internet.

If the game ever stops unexpectedly, it keeps a short note of what happened on
your iPhone and shows it to you. You can clear it, and it reaches the studio
only if you choose to email it to support yourself.
