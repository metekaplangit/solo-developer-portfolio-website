Trace letters on a wheel to spell words, and each one you find drops into the
crossword above it. Fill the crossword and the journey moves on to its next
stop.

## Three long journeys

Waypost Words is played as travel: three journeys, each of twenty places and
five hundred stops. The first is open from the start.

## Every word counts

A word that fits no answer in the crossword still goes somewhere. It lands in
the bonus jar, and a full jar pays out.

## Something new each day

A daily puzzle, a daily gift, and a few daily goals sit beside the journeys.
