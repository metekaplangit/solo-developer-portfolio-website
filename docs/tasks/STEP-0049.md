# Task Card — STEP-0049

- **ID:** STEP-0049
- **Type:** feature (UI — cross-route consistency; phase closer)
- **One outcome:** One rail, one type ramp, one button set across all eight routes, proved by measurement rather than by eye — the owner's "everything nicely aligned, positioned and well played".
- **Why now:** Sixth and last card of the makeover, and the only one that could not run earlier: drift between page families is invisible until every family has been converted.
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) At every measured width on every route, the left edge of the page's `h1` and of the footer's first column equal the nav wordmark's. (2) Every product name is the same size wherever a product leads a surface. (3) Every button on the site is a member of the declared height set. (4) 0 horizontal overflow. (5) The findings and the fixes are recorded, not just the pass.
- **Method:** a scripted pass in a contained browser over **8 routes × 7 widths = 56 page-states** (320/390/430/744/1024/1280/1440), asserting rail equality for `h1` and the footer, button-height membership in {36, 44, 52}px, and `scrollWidth === clientWidth`; plus a computed-`font-size` pass over every `h1` and every `.product-title` at 1440.
- **What the measurement found — three real defects, all fixed here:**
  1. **The product's own name was the one heading not on the shared rail.** On `/apps/sole-focus/` the `h1` started at **173px (1024)** and **227px (1440)** while the nav wordmark, the footer and every other `h1` started at 51 / 99 — the avatar sat in a left grid column and pushed it in. The head now stacks at every width.
  2. **The policy title had the same defect, worse.** On `/privacy/sole-focus/` the `h1` started at **108px at 390px wide** — badly inboard on a phone — and 147 / 195 at 1024 / 1440. Same cause, same fix.
  3. **The product name rendered at 32px on its own page and 60px in every band.** Its scoped `font-size: var(--display-3)` was not reaching the built stylesheet (no matching rule with a `font-size` existed in the served CSS, confirmed by walking `document.styleSheets`). Rather than chase the scoping, the size moved to a global `.product-title`, which is also what stops the three surfaces drifting again.
- **Design decision — the ramp lives in global rules, not in components.** `.page-title` (STEP-0048) and `.product-title` (this card) are global classes. A per-component `font-size` is exactly how four different `h1` sizes accumulated in the first place, and defect 3 above is a scoped rule silently not applying. One definition each, referenced everywhere.
- **Non-goals:** new structure, new content, new colour. This card only removes drift; anything found that needed a design decision would have become its own card.
- **Open item, deliberately not taken:** the brand mark (`public/favicon.svg` and the derived icon/OG set) is still periwinkle and is now the only chromatic element in the studio chrome. Regenerating the mark and every derived asset is its own outcome, not a drift fix. Recorded in STEP-0045 and left open here.
