# Task Card — STEP-0081

- **ID:** STEP-0081
- **Type:** fix (alignment)
- **One outcome:** Every page title that leads with the identity lockup starts at the same distance from the site's shared left rail.
- **Feedback:** owner, 2026-08-04 — a UI polish pass over sizing, alignment and placement. The choice between the two possible fixes was the owner's own, taken at planning: keep the icon beside the title, and make the two indents match.
- **Why now:** it is the one alignment inconsistency the measurement pass found, and it is the last card in the batch.
- **What it actually was:** measured at 320 / 390 / 768 / 1440, the wordmark, the first body paragraph and the first footer link share one left edge on all 9 routes (20 / 20 / 38.4 / 98.5px). Six routes start their `h1` on that rail. The two that lead with the identity lockup do not, and they disagreed with each other: **+88px** on the product pages and **+72px** on the policy pages at 768px and above (84 / 68 below). The lockup pushes the title in by the icon's width plus the gap, so an icon size *is* a title indent — and the product header used 72px while the policy header used 56px. Under CHECKLIST L2 that is one component showing two treatments; under L3 it is a title that does not follow the grid.
- **The decision, and why it went this way:** 56 everywhere, not 72. The policy header's 56 has a written reason on the component — policy titles are the longest headings on the site, and 72 towered over a two-line block on a phone. The product header's 72 had none. Given one size had a recorded rationale and the other did not, the one with the reason wins. 56 is also what `ProductBand` already uses, so the site now has two identity sizes, not three: **48 in a card, 56 wherever a product leads a surface.** A scale, rather than drift.
- **Not the other option:** putting every title on the rail would mean moving the icon off the title's line. That was tried and reversed in STEP-0061 on the owner's explicit instruction — "it should be on the left side of the text of the headline, not on two different lines wasting space" — and the reversal note is still in `global.css`. The owner chose the matching indent at planning; this card does not reopen it.
- **Avoid:** inventing a third icon size to split the difference, and touching the `.identity` lockup itself, which four surfaces share.
- **Approach:** one number changes — the product detail header's `ProductAvatar` goes from `size={72}` to `size={56}`. Everything else follows from the shared lockup. A new assertion in `tests/geometry.test.ts` then holds it: across every route at a given width, the set of non-zero title indents must have at most one member.
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) the product and policy headers report the same title indent at every width. (2) The six routes whose titles sit on the rail are unchanged. (3) The assertion is seen red against the previous build first. (4) No change to the `.identity` lockup, to `ProductBand`, or to `ProductCard`.
- **Non-goals:** moving any title onto the rail (settled in STEP-0061), the `h2` positions, and the card size of 48.
