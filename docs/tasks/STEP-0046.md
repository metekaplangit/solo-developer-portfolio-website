# Task Card — STEP-0046

- **ID:** STEP-0046
- **Type:** feature (UI — home page structure)
- **One outcome:** The home page becomes the Spectrum poster: a tall studio hero, a hue-painted product band, three statements in the product's own approved words, a mosaic of every remaining real screenshot, the promise band, and the maker note.
- **Why now:** Third card of six in the owner-selected makeover. Today's home page shows one of five real screenshots and puts the product below a claim with no evidence in it; the identity swap (STEP-0045) re-coloured it but left that structure intact.
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) Bands as designed, `--hue` resolved through `productHue()` rather than hardcoded. (2) **All five** real screenshots appear on the page. (3) Every string comes from the content file or `site.ts`, except the headline settled by the owner. (4) Hero height capped so a tall window cannot produce a wall of nothing. (5) 0 horizontal overflow across the width matrix. (6) Heading order `h1 → h2 → h3` intact.
- **Owner decision — the headline:** `site.headline` ("Brilliant Products for All") is replaced by **"Small studio. / Serious craft."**, chosen by the owner on 2026-07-31 from four options. The old value stays in `site.ts` unused rather than being deleted, so reverting is one line.
- **Design decision — the hero may carry the product's hue, conditionally:** One Voice Per Band says a hue belongs to a product's band. With exactly **one** featured product the whole page is that product's band, so the hero carries its colour; `heroOwnsHue` re-evaluates to false the moment a second product is featured, at which point the colour would no longer belong to anything in particular. The condition is in the code, not in a comment.
- **Design decision — `bleed` instead of a `100vw` bleed:** `BaseLayout` gained a `bleed` prop that drops the shared `.container` from `<main>`, and bands carry `.rail` inside. The usual `margin-inline: calc(50% - 50vw)` trick was rejected: `scrollbar-gutter: stable` reserves the scrollbar, so `100vw` exceeds the client width and produces horizontal page overflow.
- **Design decision — three statements come from the prose, not from a new field:** `src/lib/prose.ts` reads `## Heading` sections out of the product's Markdown body. Duplicating approved copy into frontmatter so a page could show it would create two sources for one sentence. The helper is pure and unit-tested; writing those tests caught a real defect (a body whose first line is a heading returned the whole document as "intro") before the page used it.
- **Non-goals:** the catalog and detail pages (STEP-0047); the reading routes (STEP-0048); cross-route alignment (STEP-0049); any invented metric, review or logo.
- **Files:** `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro`, `src/styles/global.css` (`.rail`), `src/lib/prose.ts`, `src/lib/prose.test.ts`.
- **Evidence method:** per-width overflow sweep in a contained browser + full-page capture reviewed at 1440.
- **Proof classification:** machine-verifiable + manual-runtime (measured).
