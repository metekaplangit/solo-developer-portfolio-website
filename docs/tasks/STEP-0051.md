# Task Card — STEP-0051

- **ID:** STEP-0051
- **Type:** feature (UI polish — component consolidation)
- **One outcome:** The small pill used for badges, the policy at-a-glance row and the gallery's position counter is defined once and used three times, instead of being defined three times.
- **Why now:** Measured 2026-07-31 — `padding: 0.25rem 0.6rem` appeared verbatim in `Badge.astro`, `PolicyArticle.astro` and `ScreenshotShowcase.astro`. This is the same failure the card system fixed in STEP-0042 (four definitions of one card), one scale down, and it is what lets chips drift apart the next time one is touched. Second of four polish cards.
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) One definition — `.chip` in `global.css` — with the tones the three call sites need. (2) All three render through it. (3) Computed padding and radius identical at all three sites. (4) No visual change beyond the two deliberate corrections below. (5) `grep "0.25rem 0.6rem" src/` returns no rule.
- **Design decision — shape is shared, size is a named modifier.** The at-a-glance row is deliberately larger and bolder than a metadata badge, so `.chip-lg` keeps that difference rather than flattening the policy pages to make a table of numbers match.
- **Design decision — extend the shared class, not a fourth component.** `.chip` is global rather than a component because two of the three call sites are single elements inside tight markup (a list item, an absolutely-positioned counter); wrapping those in a component would cost more than it saves.
- **Two deliberate corrections, made while consolidating:**
  1. **The neutral badge carried a fill AND a `--border` hairline**, breaking the Single-Cue Rule the policy chips were already following — and `PolicyArticle` even carried a comment saying so. Settled in favour of the fill. `.chip-glass` is now the single documented exception, because it sits over arbitrary imagery where a fill alone cannot separate it.
  2. **The gallery counter's fill was a hardcoded `rgba(22, 23, 27, 0.6)`** — the *retired* graphite ground `#16171b` at 60%, left behind by the STEP-0045 palette swap and invisible against the new near-black. It now mixes from `--bg`, so it cannot go stale again.
- **Padding moved onto the space scale:** `0.25rem 0.6rem` → `var(--space-1) var(--space-3)` (4px 12px, was 4px 9.6px). Chips widen by ~2.4px a side. This is the one visible change and it is deliberate: `0.6rem` was an invented value, and the extra width also lifts every chip clear of the 24px target minimum (measured 30–33px tall).
- **Avoid:** flattening the two sizes into one, and touching the gallery's markup beyond the counter's class list.
- **Non-goals:** the gallery's behaviour, layout or controls; the policy chip logic; STEP-0052 and STEP-0053.
- **Files:** `src/styles/global.css` (`.chip` and tones), `src/components/Badge.astro`, `src/components/PolicyArticle.astro`, `src/components/ScreenshotShowcase.astro`.
- **Evidence method:** computed-style equality across the three call sites.
- **Proof classification:** machine-verifiable (measured), with one item recorded **unproven** below.
- **Required checks:** `npm run build`; `npm run check`; `npm test`; validator; deploy-time Lighthouse a11y gate (≥0.95).
- **Unproven, stated rather than rounded up:** the counter's *live update* could not be observed. It is driven by an `IntersectionObserver` (`ScreenshotShowcase.astro:386`), and IO callbacks do not fire in the browser harness available on this machine — independently established earlier the same day while building the mockup round, which is why a different component there was rewritten off IO. Four probes were run (dot click, arrow click, direct `scrollLeft`, synthetic `scroll` event), on both a hidden iframe and a fronted page, and the counter stayed at `1 / 5` in all of them. The change cannot plausibly be the cause — it adds two classes to the counter's parent `<p>`, while the observer watches `.slide` elements and `render()` writes to `.counter-now`, none of which were touched — but "cannot plausibly" is reasoning, not evidence, so it is recorded as unproven. **Worth an eye on the live page after deploy.**
- **RESOLVED, same day, after v0.44.2 deployed:** driven on the **live site**
  (`https://metkapstudio.com/apps/sole-focus/`) the counter moved `1 / 5` →
  `2 / 5` on the next-arrow and the active dot advanced to index 1. The gallery
  is intact; the four failed probes were the harness, exactly as suspected —
  IntersectionObserver does not deliver callbacks in the local browser pane on
  this machine. Chip padding confirmed live at `4px 12px`. Nothing to fix.
