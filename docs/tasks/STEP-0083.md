# Task Card — STEP-0083

- **ID:** STEP-0083
- **Type:** feature (home spotlight) + fix (colour system)
- **One outcome:** Magic Notes leads the home page, and the page it leads reads as Magic Notes.
- **Feedback:** owner, 2026-08-17 — "yes, make magic notes the featured app", answering the open decision STEP-0082 recorded.
- **Why now:** the owner asked, and STEP-0082 deliberately left it as their call rather than doing it quietly.
- **What it actually was:** `featured` is one boolean per product, and exactly one product may hold it — `index.astro` gives the hero a product's hue only while `featured.length === 1`. So the swap is two edits: Magic Notes takes it, Sole Focus gives it up. The swap itself is trivial. **What it exposed is not:** the home page's bands had only ever been fed one product's content, and three things broke the moment a different one arrived.
- **Finding 1 — every band on the site wore Sole Focus's orange.** `--hue-soft` and `--hue-wash` are made from `--hue`, and a custom property substitutes its `var()` references on the element that **declares** it, not on the element that uses it. Both were declared on `:root`, so both baked in the `#ff9245` fallback and every band painted that colour whatever product owned it. Measured on the built page: `.product-band` computed `--hue: #B2BBC5` and painted `oklab(0.7635 0.0960 0.1267 / 0.16)`, which is #ff9245. **Latent for eleven months** because one product owned the only hue on the site and it happened to be the fallback. DESIGN.md §2 said the opposite, and the comment directly above `.panel-accent` said "the wash is the product's hue, never the site's".
- **Finding 2 — a lone screenshot at the bottom of the page.** The mosaic is `shots.slice(1)` in a two-column grid. Sole Focus's 5 shots gave 1 lead + 4, two full rows. Magic Notes has 6, so the swap left a fifth figure alone beside a 604px empty cell — the raggedness STEP-0063 banned in the feature grid, arriving in a different band.
- **Finding 3 — one statement column twice the height of the others.** The says band renders the opening sentence of each of the first three body sections. Sole Focus's were written to a matching length (STEP-0062 records it). Magic Notes' second section opened with a 34-word list: measured at 1440, the three columns were **105 / 204 / 105px**.
- **The decisions, and why they went this way:**
  - Finding 1 is fixed **once**, in `global.css`, by declaring the two derived washes on the four surfaces that set `--hue` — not by re-declaring them at each inline `style` (four call sites that a fifth surface would silently miss).
  - Finding 2 is fixed **generally**, by rounding the mosaic down to an even count. Sole Focus is unaffected (4 either way) and a product with a seventh shot cannot reopen it.
  - Finding 3 is fixed in **copy, not code**: the long list sentence gains a seven-word lead, "It reads the arithmetic people actually write." Truncation was rejected in STEP-0062 for reading as a page that ran out of room, and picking sections by sentence length would be arbitrary. **This edits owner-approved copy from the app's own repository** — restating what the page's opening paragraph already says, adding no capability claim — and is called out for the owner rather than slipped in.
- **Not fixed, and it needs the owner:** the home page's maker-note band is now absent. It renders from `makerNote`, which Magic Notes does not have, because a first-person note is published only with the maker's explicit approval of the exact words (schema comment, STEP-0035). Nothing here can write it. The page closes on "The deal" instead, which reads as an ending rather than a hole.
- **Avoid:** writing a maker's note; touching `--hue: #ff9245` itself, which is a documented fallback and is still correct as one; and reordering `PRODUCT_STATUSES` or the sort, which is what decides the spotlight when two products are featured.
- **Approach:** two `featured` flags, one CSS rule, one `slice`, one sentence, and two new pins.
- **Acceptance criteria:** (1) exactly one product featured, and it is Magic Notes. (2) Every surface that owns a hue paints its washes in that hue, on every route at every width. (3) The mosaic ends on a full row. (4) The three statement columns are level at 1440. (5) The hue assertion seen red against the previous CSS first. (6) Sole Focus's own page and card are unchanged.
- **Non-goals:** a maker's note; the 768px reflow of the says band, where one column takes an extra line because the grid drops to two columns — a wrap, not a defect; whether Sole Focus should return to the spotlight later.
