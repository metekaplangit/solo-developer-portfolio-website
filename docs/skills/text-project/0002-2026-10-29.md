# Text project 0002

## The product

- **What it is**: MetKap Studio's website, a static Astro 7 site for 3 products (Magic Notes, Sole Focus, Waypost Words), with About, Support, Privacy and 404.
- **For**: the indie-Mac-app visitor deciding whether the software is good and the person behind it trustworthy (`PRODUCT.md`).
- **Platform**: the web. No platform record for the site; `PRODUCT.md` says `web`.
- **Standard sizes this run**: 1920, 1536 and 414 wide. StatCounter, September 2026, read this run: desktop 1920×1080 28.07%, 1536×864 10%, 1366×768 7.95% ([desktop](https://gs.statcounter.com/screen-resolution-stats/desktop/worldwide)); mobile 414×896 13.35%, 360×800 7.54%, 390×844 6.05% ([mobile](https://gs.statcounter.com/screen-resolution-stats/mobile/worldwide)). Report 0001 used 1440 and 390; this run's figures put 1536 and 414 ahead, so they replace them. Smallest claimed: 320, judged for fit only.
- **Instrument limit**: headless Chrome keeps an 11px scroll bar, so the 1536 and 1920 layouts are 1525 and 1909 px wide. A Mac with overlay scroll bars gets the full width.
- **Languages**: English only. Strings live in `src/content/products/*.md`, `src/lib/site.ts` and the `.astro` pages, written in the code. No translation table, so no growth check applies.
- **Type**: `src/styles/global.css` — fluid scale; `text-wrap: balance` on `h1–h3`, `p` and `li`; `pretty` only inside `.sec-body`. `tie()` in `src/lib/typography.ts` and `src/lib/satteri-tie.ts` (Markdown) join short words to the next with a no-break space.
- **Voice**: plain, first person, calm, exact. 3 lines that show it:
  - "Support goes straight to me."
  - "Small studio. Serious craft."
  - "I usually reply within 2–3 business days."

## The feeling

- Home, the Sole Focus page and About carry it most fully: near-black stage, one hue per product, large calm type, short sentences.
- Feeling: calm, exact, personal, unhurried.
- Type, space and voice belong together through restraint; the weak lines are the ones that talk more than they show.
