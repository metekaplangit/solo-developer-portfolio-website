## The product

- **What it is**: MetKap Studio's website, a static Astro 7 site showing 3 products (Magic Notes, Sole Focus, Waypost Words), with About, Support, Privacy and 404.
- **For**: people deciding whether to get one of the apps from the Mac App Store or App Store.

- **Screens judged**: 1920 and 1440 desktop, 390 phone. - **Voice**: plain, first person, calm, exact. 3 lines that show it:
  - "Support goes straight to me."
  - "Small studio. Serious craft."
  - "I usually reply within 2–3 business days."
