# Inspect: what on the site's screens looks wrong

## The bar

A finding earns a card only if a visitor would feel it, pay for it, lose data to it, or be stopped by it; a WCAG 2.2 floor missed on screen always clears it. A small accident below the bar is fixed in a small-fix card. Anything else below the bar is a line here.

## Scope, surfaces and price

- Nothing typed with the command: every screen, the normal run.
- Platform record: none. The product is a static website, so the floors are WCAG 2.2 and the browser.
- Surfaces: the project's own headless route (Playwright driving Chrome, GPU drawing: "ANGLE Metal Renderer: Apple M1 Pro"). No brokered surface was opened; the browser pane never paints for this project, so it could not answer anything here.
- Price, measured before the sweep: one tall phone page 3.5 s quiet; 184 cells in 3 lanes took about 4 minutes a pass. Mean cell 2.9 s, slowest 13.2 s, under a load of about 7 on 10 cores.
- The sweep: `scripts/probe/inspect-probe.mjs` (new; its header says how to run it).
- Earlier reports: none of this skill. Handoffs read: improve 0001, smooth 0001, hunt 0001, chaos 0001; inspect and evaluate have no folder.

Instrument traps found this run:

- A Playwright full-page picture drops the phone's coarse pointer for the rest of the page: gallery dots fell from 44 to 28 px and everything below moved up 28 px. Pass 1's pictures and pixel contrast were thrown away and the whole sweep re-run with window-sized pictures.
- The non-text reader read black for marks drawn by a pseudo-element or a stroke; it now reads the mark from pixels.
- `naturalWidth` of a `srcset` picture is its `sizes` length, not its pixels; sharpness was read from the files in `dist/` instead.
- A "worst nearby pixel" contrast reading picks up neighbouring text; the ground the text sits on (the median) is the verdict.
- Lazy pictures parked sideways in a rail never load; waiting on them cost 4 s a page until they were left out.
