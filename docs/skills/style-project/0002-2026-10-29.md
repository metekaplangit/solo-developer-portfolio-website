# Style Project 0002

## The product

- **What it is:** MetKap Studio's public site — a static Astro site for 3 shipped products (Magic Notes, Sole Focus, Waypost Words), their privacy pages and support. Web only, English only. The root instruction file has no platform record; the build targets the web.
- **Design language:** `DESIGN.md` ("Spectrum") used as the boundary. Every redesign below sits inside it. The code wins where they disagree:
  - `DESIGN.md`'s chips section still names "Periwinkle/Signal Wash"; the code has no such colour — chips take each product's own hue.
- **Values as they really are** (tokens in `src/styles/global.css` `:root`; components read them):
  - Stage `#08090c`, band `#0f1015`, surfaces `#171b1f` / `#1e2027`; text `#f2f3f6`, muted `#a2a7b3`, faint `#8b909c`.
  - 1 hue per product: Sole Focus `#ff9245`, Magic Notes `#b2bbc5` (Graphite, achromatic on purpose — STEP-0069), Waypost Words `#c6b4e8`. The site has no colour of its own.
  - Hairlines white 9% / 18%. Shadows all fall downward; lifted surfaces carry a 1px top highlight.
  - Radii 10 / 18 / 24 / pill; icon masks in %.
  - Type: system SF; sizes 13/15/16/17/20/24/32/40/60/68/112; weights 400/600/700; mono only for the support email and the 404 code.
  - Blur: header 16px + saturate, gallery glass 8px.
- **Modes:** dark only. Increased contrast honoured through `prefers-contrast: more` (hairlines 9% → 32%, 18% → 56%, greys raised).
- **Way in:** static HTML, no launch screen, `theme-color #08090c`; the browser restores scroll on reopen.
- **Feeling:** quiet, exacting, near-black stage, colour lent by each product. Family: Apple-native dark — SF, tonal surfaces, pill controls.
  - Home — hero type and the Magic Notes poster band.
  - Apps — the 3 bands stacked, each in its own hue.
  - Sole Focus — orange voice, gallery, ledger.
  - Waypost Words — lavender voice, phone row.
  - Support — cards and tiles.

### 1 — Product voices

- **Why it cleared the bar:** the Magic Notes button is the main action on 3 screens and looks switched off.
- **Brief:**
  - Weak: Graphite fills read as a disabled grey; Sole Focus's wash is louder than the others.
  - Premium: every product's button is equally alive in its own hue; washes share 1 lightness.
  - References: Rogue Amoeba's per-product buttons; grey-reads-disabled sources; OKLCH shared lightness.
  - Keep: no site colour; each product lends its own.
- **Rounds:**
  - 1 (`voice`): lit fills (top +.09 L, foot −.09 L, inset top highlight) on `.btn-hue` and `.btn-primary`; washes at L .62, chroma clamped .03–.1. Missed the product-page Download, which is `btn-primary` with the hue as accent.
  - 2 (`voice2`, kept): `--accent-fill` carries the lit fill into the product page.
- **Made ours:** Rogue Amoeba fills flat, saturated buttons from the icon; here the hue stays the product's and the light is the site's — a lit aluminium face that also works on an achromatic hue.
- **After:** Graphite button reads as lit metal, orange and lavender lit the same way. Lowest contrast unchanged 5.33. Mean pixel change: home 3.59, apps 2.23, Magic Notes 1.45.

### 2 — Screenshot frames

- **Why it cleared the bar:** the screenshots are the products; 4 frame recipes make the same object look cut from different sites.
- **Brief:**
  - Weak: corners 18 or 24; ring, border or nothing; 3 shadow depths.
  - Premium: 1 frame wherever a screenshot sits, inner corner nested.
  - References: Raycast's single image frame with a lit top edge; App Store screenshot framing guides.
  - Keep: quiet, near-black stage, nothing louder than the product.
- **Rounds:**
  - 1 (`frames`): 1 radius, 1 ring, medium shadow. All radii 24, frame borders 0 — but invisible (home 0.2).
  - 2 (`frames2`): large shadow and a lit edge drawn over the picture. Still invisible (home 0.06, Waypost 5.87).
  - 3 (`frames3`, kept): a 6px lit mat — a plate lighter at the top — with the picture's corner nested inside (24 − 6 = 18).
- **Made ours:** Raycast frames with a hairline and highlight on the image; here the screenshot sits in a mount, lit from above like every other lifted surface on the site.
- **After:** 1 recipe on band shots, band phones, gallery and phone row; outer 24, inner 18, nesting exact; frame borders 40+8 → 0. The home mosaic keeps its outline only. Mean pixel change: home 5.94, Waypost 11.39, Sole Focus 3.59. Lowest contrast unchanged.

### 3 — Chips

- **Why it cleared the bar:** a row of 3 chips under every product name reads as 3 different parts.
- **Brief:**
  - Weak: weights 400 and 600 side by side; fills differ by what sits behind; the quiet chip is a stray word.
  - Premium: 1 chip, 3 tiers told apart by fill alone.
  - References: Linear's single chip; Red Hat tag guidance; chip/badge pattern articles.
  - Keep: small, quiet, the product's hue on the first chip.
- **Rounds:** 1 (`chips`, kept). Product tint / neutral wash / outline; 1 weight.
- **Made ours:** Linear's chips are 1 grey look; here the first chip carries the product's own hue and the third is an outline, so the row reads as a ranked set.
- **After:** 11 chips moved from 400 to 600; quiet chips gain a 1px 18% hairline (4); neutral fill `#1e2027` → white 8% wash. Small on the page (apps 0.04) but plain on the chip row itself.

### 4 — Small labels

- **Why it cleared the bar:** 66 tracked caps on every route make the quiet site shout in small type.
- **Brief:**
  - Weak: eyebrows, ledger keys and "For you if" all in caps at 0.08em.
  - Premium: small labels set like the rest — sentence case, strong weight, no tracking.
  - References: Things, Raycast and Linear all use some caps labels; caps-tracking craft notes.
  - Keep: the label's role — smaller, stronger, quieter than body.
- **Rounds:** 1 (`labels`, kept). New tokens `--label-size/-weight/-track/-case`; 8 files point at them.
- **Disagrees with the research:** all 3 references keep caps labels. Followed this product's feeling instead — on a site this quiet the caps are the loudest thing at small size. The tokens make caps 1 line back if wanted.
- **Made ours:** the references track caps; here labels are sentence case, so they read as words, not stamps.
- **After:** tracked caps 66 → 0. Lowest contrast unchanged 5.33. Mean pixel change: home 1.65, apps 0.74.

### 5 — Band edges

- **Why it cleared the bar:** each product band opens on a hard horizontal line, so the catalogue reads as stacked panels.
- **Brief:**
  - Weak: stage `#08090c` straight to band `#0f1015`, hue glow cut flat along it.
  - Premium: light falls on 1 stage; no seam.
  - References: Linear's transparent sections; Things' flat tones; section-blend guides.
  - Keep: each band's hue glow and its lift.
- **Rounds:** 1 (`bands`, kept). A top layer fades each band in from the stage over `--band-rise`.
- **Made ours:** Linear has no section tone at all; here each band keeps its own glow and only the seam goes.
- **After:** hard seams 4 → 0 (apps 3, home 1). Mean pixel change: apps 1.55, home 0.38.
- **Under the 2-second bar:** the change is real and measured, but a seam on near-black is not something a user spots in 2 seconds. No further round would make it louder without changing the bands' look.
