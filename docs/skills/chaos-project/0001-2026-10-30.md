# Chaos 0001 — where the site stops working

## Scope, blast radius, price of a proof

- Scope: the whole site; nothing typed alongside the command.
- Blast radius: the built `dist/` served by the driver on a free local port. No live site, no account, no user data; every other origin answered by a stub page. The tree was clean and pushed at 9bb22cd before the first wave. Abort condition: any write outside `scripts/probe/`, `docs/` or scratch.
- Proof costs: `npm run headless` 11 s, green before this run (exit 0). Screens suite about a minute. The driver: a storm of 3,000 events 2.5 to 5 minutes; every other wave 0.5 to 2 minutes per engine.
- Impact bar: a visitor would feel it, be misled, or be stopped — a control that does not do what was pressed, a page they cannot read or leave, an error they see.
- Surfaces opened: the app's browser pane once at the start (card used; nothing driven there). Everything else headless.
- Handoffs read: hunt-project 0001, smooth-project 0001, improve-project 0001; the other 3 audit folders do not exist.

## Research

- Own work: `search-history.sh` found Spellbent's `scripts/chaos.mjs`; its seeded mulberry32, swarm configurations and per-surface counting were rewritten here. Nothing copied.
- Internet: gremlins.js seeds through Chance.js and loads as a Playwright init script (github.com/marmelab/gremlins.js) — a seeded own driver was kept instead, since it must log exact coordinates for replay. Cross-document view transitions are skipped on back/forward cache restores and redirects (bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=280703). Interrupted momentum in a scroll-snap container can stop rendering or leave it mid-way in WebKit (bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=262287) — consistent with what wave 2 found on the rail.

- The fix: each gallery and the rail remember where the last press sent them and aim the next press from there; the rail pages to whole shots with `scrollTo`; a resize mid-glide lands on the picture the glide was going to; the visitor's own pointer, touch, sideways wheel or other key lets the remembered target go.
- Unproven: a real iPhone in Safari. WebKit through Playwright only.
- Chrome finishes any scripted smooth scroll even when a wheel arrives during it — the same with a bare `scrollTo` and none of the site's code — so "hand beats press" was proved in WebKit; in Chrome the hand wins once the glide ends.
