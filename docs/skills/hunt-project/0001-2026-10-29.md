## Candidates, and what reproduction showed

### C1 — a page change throws "Transition was aborted … ViewTransition opt-in disabled" (not reproduced as a product fault)

- Steps: serve `dist/`, open `/` in puppeteer-launched Chrome 156 at 1440, click header links in turn (`/apps/ /privacy/ /support/ /about/ /` twice), 1.5 s apart.
- Observed: 5 of 12 clicks raised that page error and arrived without a transition; with the site's one inline `pagereveal` script stripped from every page, the errors still came (9 of 17), so the script is not the source. 1 click in each run never navigated within 30 s, which ended both runs.
- Second instrument: the same clicks through Playwright, 40 in Chrome 156 and 40 in WebKit 26.4, at pauses of 2000, 600, 300 and 100 ms: 0 dead clicks, 0 page errors.
- Verdict: unproven. It appears only under puppeteer's launch of Chrome, and nowhere a visitor's browser runs. Recorded as an instrument trap.

### C2 — clicking the last gallery dot leaves the gallery between slides (not reproduced)

- Drive read the gallery 700 ms after clicking dot 6 from slide 1 at 1440 and found no slide centred, counter 5.
- Repro read the same click at 300, 700, 1500 and 3000 ms: Sole Focus counter 2 → 5 → 5 (slide 5 of 5) → 5; Magic Notes 2 → 5 → 6 → 6. The smooth scroll across 5 slides takes up to 1.5 s and lands right.
- Verdict: the probe read mid-scroll. Waits raised to 2 s for dot jumps, 0.9 s per arrow.

### C3 — resizing on slide 3 jumps the gallery to the last slide (not reproduced)

- Drive: after the C2 dot click, slide 3 then a resize 1440 → 390 showed slide 6.
- Repro with the scroll settled first: slide 3 stayed slide 3 for 1440 → 390, 390 → 1440, 1180 → 820 (an iPad turning) and 1440 → 1000, on both galleries, counter matching.
- Verdict: a knock-on of C2's unfinished scroll in the probe.

### C4 — the skip-link step could not run (driver fault)

### C5 — in WebKit, a resize while a gallery rests on slide 3 moves it to another slide (REPRODUCED — finding 1)

- Steps: serve `dist/`; in Playwright WebKit 26.4 open `/apps/sole-focus/` at 932×900 (an iPhone on its side); scroll the gallery into view; click dot 3 (or Next twice); wait 2.5 s; set the viewport to 430×900 (the same iPhone upright); wait 2.5 s; read which slide is centred and the counter.
- Expected: slide 3, counter 3 — the picture the visitor was looking at.
- Observed: slide 5 of 5, counter 5 (scrollLeft 1674 of 837 before, 1540 of 385 after). Same at 844 → 390 (slide 5) and 1440 → 390 (Sole Focus slide 5, Magic Notes slide 6). Unmoved: 1440 → 1000, 1180 → 820, 820 → 1180, 430 → 932, 390 → 1440, and Magic Notes at 932 → 430.
- Chrome 156, same steps, all 20 cases: slide 3 stays slide 3.
- Repro scripts: the probe's gallery step (`hunt-probe.mjs drive --engine webkit`), plus the throwaway `wk-resize.mjs` / `wk-rotate.mjs` of this run.
- Cause: the gallery has no code for a size change — it trusts the browser to keep the snapped slide. WebKit keeps the old pixel offset; when the narrower track's furthest scroll is shorter than that offset, it clamps to the end and snaps there, so the slide that was showing is lost. Every case that moved is exactly one where `(slide − 1) × old width` exceeds `(slides − 1) × new width`; every case that held is one where it does not. Chrome follows the snapped element instead. Closest WebKit reports: bug 184774 (a snapping container's scroll width changing leaves the position wrong) and the re-snap-after-layout fix of bug 255492, which this case evidently does not reach.
- Chain: the counter and dots follow the scroll, so they agree with the wrong slide — nothing on the page says anything went wrong. Both slide galleries share the one `initGallery`, so both products' pages inherit it; the phone rail pages by view and is not affected (drive: matches).

### C6 — in WebKit, the first Tab lands on the gallery, not the skip link (intended)

- Plain Tab in Safari skips links unless the visitor turns on "Press Tab to highlight each item"; Option-Tab landed on the skip link first. Platform behaviour, not the site's.
