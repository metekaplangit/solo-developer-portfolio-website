## Research (read 2026-10-28)

- Best-made products: apple.com MacBook Pro page, its `overview.built.css` read this run: paddle arrows 36px circles, dots 8px with 8px margins, current dot widens into a timed pill. Volvo Cars design system pagination (designsystem.volvocars.com/web/components/pagination): plain dots up to 5 items, `aria-current`, a "current / total" pill beyond. GNOME libadwaita `AdwCarouselIndicatorDots`: current dot larger and more opaque, tied to scroll position.
- Platform guidance: Apple HIG Page controls (data JSON): "A solid dot denotes the current page", no more than about 10 dots, "Center a page control at the bottom". W3C WAI-ARIA APG Carousel: prev/next don't move focus; pickers as tabs or a labelled group, current via `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`.
- Craft: Apple HIG Icons: match icon weight to adjacent text; nudge asymmetric glyphs for optical balance. Baymard, "Page Control UI": the elongated-pill current state is the current mainstream pattern; keep active and inactive distinguishable over images.
- Cost on this stack: MDN `backdrop-filter` (last modified 2026-04-20) has no performance section. Mozilla bug 1828248 says backdrop-filter is resource-intensive and scales with area and count. Followed: no new blurred element, and blur area cut by shrinking the arrow button.
- Legal: Apple App Store Marketing Guidelines ("Don't use the standalone Apple logo"; "Use only the badge artwork provided"); Apple trademark guidelines for third parties ("You may not use the Apple Logo … on or in connection with web sites"). Apple's own badge is a downloaded licensed asset, which this skill does not fetch, so piece 3 draws the studio's own mark instead.
- Research vs judgement: Apple uses 36px arrow buttons. This site's arrows float over 1150px screenshots and must stay 44px+ for touch, so 48px was kept. Followed my judgement over the reference here.

Fallback: not needed. The shortfall is in pieces, not ideas.

## The 3

Only 3 pieces sit below premium. The rest are premium for their job, belong to another skill, or (the tile chevron) are 1 line. The list is not padded to 5.

### 1. Gallery arrows (family of 4) — new

- **Bar:** the signature component's controls look like a stock carousel, with a leftover colour; every product page shows them.
- **Brief:** weak = heavy generic chevron, 4 stacked cues, retired fill. Premium = a finer chevron matched to the 600-weight labels, optically placed, in the site's own glass recipe with 1 cue more than a chip at most. References: Apple paddlenav, HIG icon weight. Keep: glass over the shot, hue on hover. Budget: ≤ 2 nodes per glyph, no added blur, ≤ 15 layers, 60 fps held.
- **Rounds:** 1 — 20-unit chevron, 2px line, 48px button, `.chip-glass` recipe, shadow removed; critique: at rest the button nearly vanished over dark shots. 2 — `--border-strong` hairline, chevron 8 × 14px; critique: reads over dark and light shots, centred 0.75px toward the point on purpose. Kept round 2.
- **Cost before → after:** glyph 1 path 2 nodes → same; ink 10.5 × 18.7 @ 2.33px → 8 × 14 @ 2px; button 56px with 4 cues → 48px with 3; blurred elements 4 → 4; layers 15 → 15; fps moving 60.5 → 60.4.
- **2-second test:** passes side by side (smaller, finer, no halo). In place it is the quietest of the 3.

### 2. Gallery dots (family) — new

- **Bar:** the position row is the first thing under every screenshot and reads as a default component.
- **Brief:** weak = 11px marks, scale-up current. Premium = smaller marks, current stretches to a pill in the product hue. References: Apple dotnav, Baymard, HIG. Budget: 1 drawn mark per dot, no filter, hit area unchanged.
- **Rounds:** 1 — 7px marks at 30% text, current 22 × 7px pill, `width` transition, switched off for reduced motion. Critique: clear, calm, hit area untouched. Kept round 1.
- **Cost before → after:** 1 mark per dot → same; 11px (current 14.9) → 7px (current 22 × 7); hit area 28/44px → same; fps 60.5 → 60.4.
- **2-second test:** passes.

### 3. Download mark (single) — new

- **Bar:** the owner would be stopped by it — Apple's guidelines rule the standalone logo out (filed as issue 0004).
- **Brief:** weak = Apple's logo, nudged into line by hand. Premium = the studio's own mark on the chevrons' grid and round ends, weight matched to the 17px label. Budget: 1 path, 2 nodes, button no wider.
- **Rounds:** 1 — arrow resting on a line, 2px; critique: lighter than the label. 2 — 2.25 units (2.1px drawn); ink centred on the label line to 0px. Kept round 2.
- **Cost before → after:** 1 filled path → 1 stroked path, 2 nodes each; ink 14.3 × 17.5 → 11.5 × 15.2; line offset 0.95px → 0px; button 213 → 212px.
- **2-second test:** passes.
- **When built:** the One-Height comment in `DownloadButton.astro` still says "the Apple mark", and `DESIGN.md` §5 Download names it too. Both change with it.
