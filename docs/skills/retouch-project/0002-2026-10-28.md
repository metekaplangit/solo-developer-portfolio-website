## The product

- MetKap Studio's site: a one-person studio's home for its Mac and iPhone apps and games, with product pages, privacy policies, support and about. Astro 7, `output: 'static'`, system fonts, no backend.
- Design language: `DESIGN.md` (Spectrum). The code agrees with it; one mismatch seen: `.panel-accent` falls back to Sole Focus orange on studio pages that set no `--hue` (filed as issue 0009).

### Feeling

- Calm, native, editorial, poster-scale, quiet.
- Carried most fully by: the home hero and product band (one hue lights one product), the apps page bands, the product gallery, the product page's ruled "What it does" list on the label rail.
- Family: Apple-native dark stage, hairlines over boxes, one coloured voice per product, big confident type.

Where research and judgement disagreed:
- Story: research leaned to a sticky label index lit in the product hue. Followed own judgement: each label stays beside its own section, not sticky — a sticky index is a second navigation the page does not need, and the CSS for it is Chrome-only.
- Opening: research says the best openings are centred. Kept the site's left-aligned label-rail opening and filled the right with real facts instead, because centring would break the rail every other band hangs on.

Fallback: not needed.

## The 5

### 1. Product story — new

- Why it clears the bar: on every product page the reader scrolls ~2000 px of prose hugging the left while the right 45% sits empty; it reads as an unfinished template beside the gallery above it.
- Brief: weak — a lone prose column with no structure across the page. Premium — the story set on the same label rail as "What it does", section names on the left, prose on the right. References — Linear changelog, iA Writer, HIG layout. Keep — hairline rules, poster type, no boxes.
- Rounds: (1) built the two-track story; (2) a tall section name pushed its second paragraph down → name spans 6 rows; the maker note's full-width rule disagreed → removed; Common questions missed the rule → selector raised; (3) Waypost's story opens on a heading and sat a line low → first-child fix. Kept round 3.
- 2-second test: passes.
- After (1440): whole-page largest empty 16.8% → 6.1% (Magic Notes), 11.1% → 5.6% (Sole Focus); weight off centre −5.1% → +0.4%; page height 4898 → 4648. At 390 the story stays one column; largest empty 1.4% → 1.1%.
- Made ours: Linear puts dates in a sticky rail; here the section names sit level with their own text on the site's existing 15rem rail, unsticky, separated by hairlines — the same rail "What it does" already uses. Not the layout of the user's other apps (they are apps, not pages). Shared core: none.

### 2. Product page opening — new

- Why it clears the bar: the first screen of every product page leaves its top-right empty (24.6% of the screen) while the price, requirement and support promise hide in faint lines under the buttons.
- Brief: weak — empty top-right, facts demoted. Premium — the facts given a home that fills the corner. References — App Store facts strip, Nova's facts line, Supercharge's facts line. Keep — left-aligned rail opening, achromatic chrome.
- Rounds: (1) ruled ledger right: Price / Requires / Made by / Support; (2) the support row had dropped "usually" from the promise → restored "Usually within 2–3 business days" so it says only what is true. Kept round 2.
- 2-second test: passes.
- After (1440): first-screen largest empty 24.6% → 12.9%; first-screen inked 59.3% → 69.9%. At 390 the ledger stacks under the buttons.
- Made ours: App Store uses label-over-value cells in a strip; this is a right-hand ledger, label left and value right on hairlines, with its last rule level with the buttons. Shared core: the price and requirement facts every app page must state.

### 3. Privacy policy page — new

- Why it clears the bar: a legal page made of 10 separately lifted cards (Sole Focus) with a mono date line reads like a settings screen, not a document, and the 15 px body is smaller than the rest of the site.
- Brief: weak — boxes, mono, small body. Premium — one ruled document, titles on the rail, body at reading size. References — Panic privacy, 37signals policies, HIG grouping by lines. Keep — the site's label rail and hairlines.
- Rounds: built once and critiqued at 1440 and 390; no further round is recorded for this part (the detail of later rounds was lost when the chat was summarised).
- 2-second test: passes.
- After (1440, Sole Focus): lifted boxes 10 → 0; body 15 px → 17 px; first-screen largest empty 15.6% → 11.7%. All 3 policies change through the one component.
- Made ours: Panic and 37signals use plain stacked sections; this sets each title on the site's 15rem label rail beside its body, ruled top and bottom. Shared core: the section headings App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i) requires.

### 4. Support page — new

- Why it clears the bar: the page breaks into 4 bands at uneven gaps (40 / 120 / 40) with the address panel stranded under the hero and an orange wash that belongs to another product.
- Brief: weak — uneven rhythm, address below the fold of attention, boxes. Premium — the headline and the address as one opening, a numbered list, one rhythm. References — GOV.UK contact pattern, Rogue Amoeba support, WCAG 3.2.6. Keep — colourless studio chrome.
- Rounds: (1) address panel beside the headline with bottom alignment rose above the page-top line → (2) aligned to the top. Kept round 2.
- 2-second test: passes.
- After (1440): weight off centre −9.4% → −5.5%; gaps 40 / 120 / 40 → 72 / 72; page height 1246 → 1079.
- Made ours: GOV.UK sets the contact block apart below; here it sits beside the headline as a mono address panel, and "What to include" is a numbered ruled row of 3. Shared core: a visible address and reply time.

### 5. About page — new

- Why it clears the bar: the first thing a visitor reads about the person behind the apps leaves 22.7% of the screen empty top-right and tips 12.3% left; the values are 4 identical cards.
- Brief: weak — lopsided hero, card grid, stray orange, mono links. Premium — one grid shared by intro and principles, numbered principles, links inside the panel. References — Linear Method, Sindre Sorhus about, HIG alignment. Keep — poster headline, colourless studio chrome.
- Rounds: (1) intro right with bottom alignment rose above the page-top line → (2) one shared 5-track grid for hero and principles; (3) "Mete Kaplan" broke across lines → joined with a non-breaking space. Kept round 3.
- 2-second test: passes.
- After (1440): first-screen largest empty 22.7% → 7.7%; weight off centre −12.3% → −2.5%; right content edge 173 → 111.
- Made ours: Linear numbers its principles in a long page; here 4 sit in 2 ruled columns beside one rail label, the intro shares their grid. Shared core: none.
