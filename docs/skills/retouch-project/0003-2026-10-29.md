## The product

- MetKap Studio's site: a one-person studio's home for 2 Mac apps and 1 iPhone/iPad game, with product pages, 4 privacy policies, support and about. Astro 7, `output: 'static'`, system fonts, one dark theme.
- Tokens, as the code has them: stage `#08090c`, surfaces in even OKLCH steps, chrome achromatic, `--hue` per product band only; text sizes xs .8125 · sm .9375 · base 1.0625 · lg 1.25 · xl 1.5 · 2xl 2 · 3xl 2.75 rem, `--display-1` clamp(2.75rem, 8.5vw, 7rem), `--display-2` clamp(2.25rem, 5.5vw, 4.25rem); weights 400/600/700; `--gutter` clamp(1.25rem, 5vw, 4.5rem), `--page-top` clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem), `--band-y` clamp(3rem, 5.5vw, 4.5rem); radii 10 / 18 / 24 / pill / icon 22.37%; One Light Rule lifts `--lift-1/2/3`; the label rail `minmax(0, 15rem)` + gutter.
- Shared components: `Nav`, `Footer`, `ProductBand` (home, Apps), `ProductTile` (support, privacy), `ProductAvatar`, `ScreenshotShowcase`, `FitStatement`, `FactsLine`, `MakerNote`, `PolicyArticle`, `DownloadButton`, `Badge`, `SupportEmail`.

## The 5

### 1. The layout rule — the page opening · new

- **Bar:** on Home, Apps and the 404 the top-right of the first screen is empty, and Home shows the same store button twice before the first scroll.
- **Brief:** weak — left-only openings, one empty corner per page; premium — one two-sided opening, About's columns, lede and actions standing on the heading's last line; refs — Rogue Amoeba and Panic statements, Apple layout (leading side first); keep — poster type, achromatic chrome, the page-top line.
- **Made ours:** the studios looked at stack statement over products; this one splits name and promise on the label rail's own columns, the grid the About principles already use. Shared core: a page names itself first. Not the layout of any of the user's other projects looked at here.
- **Rounds:** 1 built (`.opening` in `global.css`, applied to 3 pages); 2 the 404 head sank 347 px because the side was taller — changed to start-aligned with the side standing on the last line only when shorter; 3 the 404 title to `--display-1` with a 5.4em measure, destinations as ruled rows.
- **After (1440):** Home first-screen empty 16.7% → 9.0%, inked 76.6% → 84.7%, page 2872 → 2756 px; Apps empty 13.7% → 5.4%, inked 79.0% → 88.5%; 404 empty 50.9% → 33.3%, weight −11.1% → −7.4%. (390): openings stack unchanged; 404 empty 40.4% → 10.5%.
- **2-second test:** passes — the empty corner fills on every page that had one.

### 2. Privacy overview — the app policies come first · offered in 0001 (the tiles), placement new

- **Bar:** a visitor opening Privacy for one app's policy finds it ≈1,860 px down, under the website's own policy.
- **Brief:** weak — the list buried at the end; premium — the list beside the opening, recognisable by icon and date; refs — Proton, Mozilla, Apple layout; keep — `ProductTile`, the ruled policy rows.
- **Made ours:** Proton puts a bullet list between intro and body; here the list stands beside the policy's own opening as the site's icon tiles, on About's columns. Shared core: listing per-product policies.
- **Rounds:** 1 built (named `aside` slot in `PolicyArticle`, used only by the overview); 2 the policy's prose-link underline ran under every tile — cleared for the list only.
- **Words:** "separate from the website policy above" loses "above". The website policy's lead still says the policies are "listed below" (true on phones, beside on laptops) — that sentence lives in `src/content/policies/global.md`, for the build to settle.
- **After (1440):** list ≈120 px from the top (was ≈1,860); first-screen empty 14.1% → 7.8%; page 2161 → 1917 px. (390): the list follows the opening, before the 9 sections; page 3369 → 3250 px.
- **2-second test:** passes.

### 3. Home — the 3 statements · new

- **Bar:** three captions float between the product and its screenshots at caption size.
- **Brief:** weak — small body, uneven air; premium — ruled list, hanging number, body size; refs — Practical Typography (space as emphasis), the About principles; keep — the product's hue on the number, 3 equal columns.
- **Made ours:** numbered-pillar blocks elsewhere box each item; here it is the site's own hairline row with the number hanging in the product's colour. Shared core: short claims side by side.
- **Rounds:** 1 built; 2 not needed — the render met the brief.
- **After (1440):** air above / below ≈146 / ≈60 → ≈98 / ≈96 px; sentence 15 → 17 px; page 2872 → 2835 px. (390): ruled rows, number beside the title; page 3422 → 3393 px.
- **2-second test:** passes, narrowly — the rules and hanging numbers are the visible change.

### 4. Product page — the story opens on its claim · offered in 0002 (maker note)

- **Bar:** the story starts on a faint caption and an unlabelled paragraph adrift in the column.
- **Brief:** weak — two openings, neither leading; premium — one thesis, then the note as a titled section; refs — Sindre Sorhus's first-person voice, Practical Typography; keep — the label rail, the two-segment rule, the note's own words.
- **Made ours:** maker stories elsewhere live in FAQs or footers; here the product's claim leads at the rail's edge and the note is ordered after it by layout alone (`display: contents` + `order`), Markdown untouched. Shared core: a product description.
- **Rounds:** 1 built; 2 the note title rendered muted (`.prose p` won) — selector strengthened, now 24 px white.
- **After (1440):** Sole Focus page 4195 → 4221 px, Magic Notes 4567 → 4610 px; note title 13 px faint → 24 px white; the claim set at 24 px across both tracks. (390): claim first, then the note.
- **2-second test:** passes.

### 5. Apps & Games — the page's last word · new

- **Bar:** the catalogue ends on one small muted line above empty page.
- **Brief:** weak — a footnote as the ending; premium — a closing statement on the bands' own columns; refs — Panic's "We're here" close, Rogue Amoeba's quick links; keep — the exact words and the link.
- **Made ours:** others close on a link list; here the words split into a heading where the product names stand and a sentence where their pictures stand. Shared core: an invitation to hear more.
- **Rounds:** 1 built at page-title size; 2 down to `--text-3xl` (a step under the h1) on the bands' 0.85/1.15 columns; 3 clamp to 32 px on phones (the title touched the right edge at 390).
- **After (1440):** 44 px heading + 20 px sentence; page 2721 → 2737 px. (390): title 32 px, 339 px wide; page 3219 → 3239 px.
- **2-second test:** passes.
