# Website text notes

## Standing decisions

- The studio speaks as WE, US, OUR, MetKap Studio. No "I", no "me", no "Mete Kaplan", no "one person".
- Never a size: no "our team", no headcount.
- Lines about the studio name no platforms. The studio is cross-platform. A product's own lines may name its own platforms.
- Buttons are very short: 1 or 2 words at most, each word starting with a capital letter.
- Every sentence is held to the writing guide (`~/.claude/writing/`). "You" for what the reader gets, "we" only for our side, and "we" kept out of most headlines. The guide says "I, not we"; the owner's WE wins.
