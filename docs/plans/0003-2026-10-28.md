## Research

- The owner's projects: `search-history.sh` found nothing reusable.
- The web:
  - MDN `text-wrap` / `text-wrap-style` (modified 2026-06-08): `balance` is
    Baseline 2024, and Chromium balances at most 6 lines.
  - MDN `:has()` (modified 2026-08-11): widely available since Dec 2023, and
    it cannot be nested inside another `:has()`.
  - Bugzilla 1670581: fallback of U+2011 to a hyphen-minus; nothing found on
    Safari/SF Pro specifically.
- The model: the redesigns are CSS and markup inside the existing Spectrum
  system, and the risk is the generic geometry tests, not the browsers.
- Disagreement: none.
