## Research

- The owner's projects: `search-history.sh` (text-wrap, balance, pretty, nbsp,
  widow, orphan, typography, tie) found Wander Words' card "Lines break where a
  reader would break them, on every screen": balance stated once, near the top,
  in `:where()`. The idea only; written fresh here.
- The web:
  - [MDN text-wrap-style](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/text-wrap-style),
    modified 2026-06-08: balance only for blocks of 6 lines or fewer in
    Chromium, 10 in Firefox; pretty for body copy, slower.
  - [Chrome, CSS text-wrap: pretty](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/css-text-wrap-pretty),
    updated 2023-10-23: pretty prevents a lone word on the last line; it does
    not handle widows.
  - Search results only (not opened): many fonts lack U+2011 and some browsers
    draw a box. The site's system font stack (SF Pro first) already renders
    U+2011 in `built‑in`, so the risk is low.
- The model: port the tested diffs as they are; risk is the dist suite's T4
  (no bound run over 30 characters), never run against the copy.
- Disagreement: the comment above `TIE_WORDS` in `src/lib/typography.ts` says
  pretty was already site-wide and did not help; the text-project measured no
  wrap rule on body text at `f1ca550`. Followed the measurement; card A
  rewrites that comment to match what ships.
