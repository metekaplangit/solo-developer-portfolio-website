## Research

- My projects: `search-history.sh` (view-transition, pagereveal, scroll-timeline, timeline-scope, carousel, indicator, shared-element) found motion-project reports in ModernEditorApp and nothing built; nothing reused.
- Web: Chrome, "Cross-document view transitions" (developer.chrome.com, updated 2024-04-14) — elements need a unique `view-transition-name` on both pages; Chrome 126, Safari 18.2, no Firefox. MDN `timeline-scope` (modified 2026-09-14) — lets an element outside a scroller use its named timeline; "limited availability". scroll-driven-animations.style "carousel-with-markers" — the marker pattern with `timeline-scope`; WebKit bug 284526 — the demo's markers misbehave on Safari. Motion-project 0001's sources stand for the rest.
- Model: all 5 are CSS-only on this stack; the risks are duplicate names on a page that shows an icon twice, a link's label travelling with its pill, and Safari's scroll-timeline edge cases.
- Disagreement: none; the official pages and the model agree, and Safari's marker bug is why D keeps today's script as the fallback.

## A — Every page's opening lands like the home hero's, and the old page leaves first

- bump: minor
- Outcome: navigating between pages, the old page fades out in 160 ms (ease-in) with header and footer still; the new page's opening (eyebrow, title, lede or summary, action) rises 12 px and fades in, 60 ms apart, 420 ms `--ease-out` — the home hero's `rise`. Arriving on any page directly (from a link or search) plays the same opening.
- Acceptance: the two pages are never both visible at full contrast mid-change; the opening rise starts once the old page has gone; every route's opening rises on direct load; reduced motion shows the page at once with no rise and no transition; Firefox (no view transitions) still shows the rise on load and never hides content.
- Touches: `src/styles/global.css:1127-1160` (view transitions), `src/pages/index.astro:395-430` (`rise`, the hero stagger), openings at `apps/index.astro:30-35`, `apps/[slug].astro:110-124`, `about/index.astro:49-52`, `support/index.astro:33-35`, `404.astro:13-18`, `PolicyArticle.astro:79-81`.
- Must keep working: the home hero entrance exactly as today; header and footer held still; the reduced-motion switch at `global.css:1153-1172`.
- Proof: `npm run headless`; Scoped @home @apps @magic-notes @about @support @privacy-sole-focus @not-found; a probe in the card's workshop (headless Chrome, `headless: 'shell'`) reading the live animation list on the new page during a navigation and the opening's opacity/transform per frame on direct load; capture.
- Prior art: Chrome's cross-document guide customises `::view-transition-old(root)` and `::view-transition-new(root)` separately.
- Avoid: animating the opening with opacity in a way that can stick at 0 — the 2026-08-01 reveal hid content when its timeline never ran (DESIGN §6).
- Constraint: premium first, built efficiently. CSS only; no navigation script.
