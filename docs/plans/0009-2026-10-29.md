## Research

- My projects: `search-history.sh` with 8 stems found nothing related.
- Web: [MDN `order`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/order) (modified 2026-04-20) — `order` changes the visual order only, never reading or tab order; [MDN `reading-flow`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/reading-flow) (2026-04-20) — limited availability, not Baseline; [MDN `display`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/display) (2026-09-21) — some browsers drop a `contents` element from the accessibility tree; [Adrian Roselli](https://adrianroselli.com/2022/07/its-mid-2022-and-browsers-mostly-safari-still-break-accessibility-via-display-properties.html) (updated 2026-01-26) — lists, buttons and tables break under `contents`, a plain div has nothing to lose.
- Model: expected the `order` reading-order risk on C before searching; the research confirmed it and ruled out `reading-flow` as the fix.
