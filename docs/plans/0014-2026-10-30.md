## A — Every press on a screenshot gallery or the phone rail moves it, however quickly they come

bump: patch. screen: @sole-focus @magic-notes @waypost-words @waypost-rail @gallery-resize.

- Outcome: on Sole Focus and Magic Notes, N presses of Next, Previous or an arrow key move the gallery N pictures (stopping at the ends) at any speed; on Waypost Words each press of the rail's arrows or keys moves one view and it always rests on a whole shot; a window resize or phone turn during a glide lands on the picture the visitor chose.
- Reason: a visitor flipping screenshots taps Next twice and moves once — the core of every product page.
- Reproduction (chaos-project 0001, Playwright, `dist/` served locally, 1440×900): Chrome 156 — 3 Next clicks 150 ms apart from picture 1 land on picture 3 (expected 4); 80 ms apart on 2; ArrowRight on the focused track the same; both galleries. WebKit 26.4 — 80 ms apart lands on 3. Rail: 2 Next clicks at once from the start land on shots 5–8 (expected 7–10, the end), both engines; from the end, 3 ArrowLeft at once rest at 1.78 / 1.82 / 1.97 shots in WebKit (3 of 3; expected 0, shots 1–4). Storm: WebKit seed 4 step 1349, rail resting at 576 px. Rotation: dot 5 clicked, then the window turned 390×844 ↔ 844×390 20 times 30 ms apart → gallery stays on picture 1, both engines.
- Cause: `initGallery` aims every press at `current ± 1` (`ScreenshotShowcase.astro:549-562`), and `current` only moves when the scroll handler sees the glide pass halfway (`:584-597`), so a press inside that window aims at the picture already being travelled to; `pin()` (`:572-580`) then snaps a resize back to that stale `current`. `initPhones.page()` (`:632`) uses `scrollBy` from the in-flight position, so a press mid-glide adds to a half-way offset, and WebKit leaves it there.
- Chain: the counter, dots and dot marker follow the scroll, so they agree with the short move — nothing on screen says a press was lost.
- Acceptance: the reproduction above, re-run by `node scripts/probe/chaos-narrow.mjs <engine> taps` and `… rail` in Chrome and WebKit, lands every case on its expected picture or shot with the counter matching; `node scripts/probe/chaos-probe.mjs --block=sequences` and `--block=races` show no gallery or rail line in either engine; a visitor's own swipe or trackpad scroll mid-glide still wins over a press made before it.
- Touches: `src/components/ScreenshotShowcase.astro` (`initGallery`, `initPhones`), `tests/screens/site.spec.ts`.
- Must keep working: arrows, dots, keys and counter on both galleries, including the dot marker that rides the scroll timeline; swiping and trackpad scrolling; the Waypost Words phone rail reaching every shot (`@waypost-rail`); a gallery keeps its picture when the window changes size (`@gallery-resize`).
- Proof: `npm run headless`; `node scripts/rendered.mjs @sole-focus @magic-notes @waypost-words @waypost-rail @gallery-resize`; the two chaos probes above in both engines.
- Prior art: aim each press from where the track is heading, and scroll to whole positions rather than by an offset; `scrollend` is in every current engine (Safari 26.2+, InfoQ 2026-04-10) with a short `scroll` debounce as the fallback. Avoid: debouncing or ignoring presses during a glide (that is the symptom made official, not a fix); `scrollBy` from a moving offset; relying on snapping to correct a target (WebKit left it unsnapped here despite bug 227246's "correct behavior"). Constraint: premium first, built efficiently — no work while nothing moves; the hunt's re-pin on resize must keep working.
