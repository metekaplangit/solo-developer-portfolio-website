## Research

- The owner's projects: `search-history.sh` (carousel, chevron, pagination,
  dots, indicator, pill, glass, arrow) matched only generic words in Spellbent
  and a retired puzzle; nothing reusable.
- The web:
  - W3C Understanding 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), updated 2026-05-11: targets
    at least 24 × 24 CSS px, or spaced so a 24px circle on each touches no other
    target.
  - Josh Comeau, "scale with pseudo-elements" (2020-05-13): animate `transform`
    rather than `width` for smoothness.
  - pqina, "Animating width and height without the squish effect": scaling a
    rounded shape squashes its corners.
  - MDN `color-mix()` (modified 2026-09-28): Baseline widely available since
    May 2023.
- The model: borrow `.chip-glass`'s recipe and the 20-unit chevrons from the
  art diff; risk is the arrow vanishing over a bright shot once the shadow goes.
- Disagreement: Comeau prefers `transform` over `width`; for the pill, scaling
  squashes the round caps, and the art run measured 60.5 → 60.4 fps with the
  `width` transition. Followed the measurement.
