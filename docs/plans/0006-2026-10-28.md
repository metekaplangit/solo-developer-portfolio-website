## Research

- The owner's projects: `search-history.sh` (squircle, radius, prefers-contrast, elevation, shadow, letter-spacing, oklch, tracking) found JigsawPuzzle2 commit `94fbf32e` (2026-10-28), "Shadows come in 4 heights from one light" — the idea of one light and a height scale; nothing copied.
- The web:
  - [tempertemper — the increased contrast media query](https://www.tempertemper.net/blog/using-the-increased-contrast-mode-css-media-query), opened: Safari has matched `prefers-contrast: more` on macOS and iOS since May 2021; the setting is Accessibility → Display → Increase contrast.
  - [WebKit bug 273996](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=273996), opened: relative colour syntax failed with `light-dark()` custom properties; fixed 2024-08-06 via bug 245970. The site uses neither `light-dark()` nor system colours.
  - [Atlassian — elevation](https://atlassian.design/foundations/elevation), search result only: raised surfaces lighter, paired with a shadow.
  - The report's own research, read 2026-10-28, stays the source for each area's values.
- The model: port each saved diff on top of the cards before it, re-render, re-measure; risk is A shifting line breaks and E's ring stacking on the lift.
- Disagreement: the report's E kept a shadow under the ring; the Single-Cue Rule in `DESIGN.md` says one cue. Followed the rule: the ring replaces the lift in that mode, as Elastic EUI's high-contrast mode turns shadows into borders.
